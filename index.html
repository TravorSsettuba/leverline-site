Common questions

Straight answers.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. We do not charge you a fee. If your finance completes, the broker or lender may pay us a commission.

Will this affect my credit?

Sending us your request does not involve a credit search. The finance provider will explain any credit check before it runs one.

Are you the lender?

No. We are an introducer: we pass your request to a finance broker or lender. All approvals, rates and terms come from them.

Can sole traders apply?

Not at the moment. We can only help UK limited companies and LLPs. Finance for sole traders and small partnerships is regulated differently, so please speak to an FCA-authorised broker.

Can I finance used equipment or a private sale?

Often, yes. Tell us the year, make, and seller in your request and we will match accordingly.
