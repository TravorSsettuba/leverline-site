Common questions

Straight answers.

Does it cost anything to apply?

No. We do not charge you a fee. If your financing closes, the financing provider may pay us a commission.

Will this affect my credit?

Sending us your request does not involve a credit pull and we never ask for your Social Security number. The financing provider will explain any credit check before it runs one.

Are you the lender?

No. We connect businesses with third-party financing providers. All approvals, rates, and terms come from the provider.

Can I finance used equipment or a private sale?

Often, yes. Tell us the year, make, and seller in your request and we will match accordingly.
