How we use your information

Last updated 12 November 2026.

Who we are

Leverline is a UK business that introduces limited companies and LLPs to finance brokers and lenders. We are the data controller for information sent through this website. You can contact us at .

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: your name, email address and phone number, your company's name, region, time trading and a rough description of its credit history, and details of the equipment you want to buy or sell. We do not ask for dates of birth, National Insurance numbers or bank details, and we do not run credit searches.

Why we use it and our lawful basis

We use it to respond to your request and to introduce your company to finance brokers or lenders who may be able to help. Our lawful bases are your consent, given when you submit the form, and our legitimate interest in running an introduction service you have asked to use.

Who we share it with

Finance brokers and lenders we introduce you to, who will tell you how they use your information, including before any credit check. Our service providers: Google (form storage and email) and GitHub (website hosting), which may process data outside the UK under their standard safeguards. We do not sell your information.

How long we keep it

Up to 24 months after your last contact with us, then we delete it. Ask us and we will delete it sooner.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to our use of it, or withdraw consent at any time. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This website does not set tracking or advertising cookies.
